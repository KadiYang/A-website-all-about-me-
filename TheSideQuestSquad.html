The Side Quest Squad (SQS)

The Side Quest Squad is an elite group of Geeks who just do random projects in tech for fun.

They are passionate about technology and love to explore new ideas and concepts outside of their main jobs or studies.

They often collaborate on side projects, share knowledge, and support each in their tech endeavors.

The group consist of 3 Members:

Kadi Yang, James Urbina, and James Sims.

(i know right? Two James's? Confusing but it's cool lol)

Their first random project was actually creating a Inventory Management System for a tech showcase. Handpicked by James Urbina, Kadi Yang joined the team to help with the UI/UX Design and Frontend Development.

James Sims handled the Backend Development, Database Management, and performing SQL queries.

While James Urbina took the role of Project Manager and handle with the API Contracts.

Together, they created a functional and user-friendly Inventory Management System that met the needs of a hypothetical business.
