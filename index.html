🧠 The Gamer's Mindset

Why does Kadi love videogames so much?

There was a period of time in her life where she was constantly escaping into the video game worlds. In retrospect, **playing video games has saved her life**. Kadi hates assignments and work. But what inspired and motivated her to get through deliverables and work hard, was the reward and satisfaction of playing video games with no stress.

Her mindset was:

“There is no point in playing video games when I have an assignment due a week from now. If I get it done now, I don't have to worry about it and can play video games stress free, all week long. There is no point playing video games and being stressed out by an assignment I haven't completed yet. By doing all my work first, I can play videogames with a clear mind and have no stress of missing deliverables.“

(Anyways, that’s what she thought when she was younger.)
